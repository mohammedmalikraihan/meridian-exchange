Forum Overview

A Working Forum for Policy and Infrastructure Leaders

Meridian Exchange is a closed-door policy and industry forum focused on digital resilience, sovereign data governance, and future infrastructure security.

The event brings together stakeholders responsible for policy, infrastructure, regulation, investment, cybersecurity, and operations.

Unlike a traditional conference, Meridian Exchange is structured around working roundtables designed to encourage direct participation and practical discussion.

Why Now?

Three Forces Converging

The intersection of geopolitical instability, infrastructure dependency, and digital sovereignty demands a new model of strategic dialogue.

01
Geopolitical Risk
Escalating tensions between major powers are creating new risks for cross-border data flows, cloud service continuity, and infrastructure access. Nations can no longer assume uninterrupted access to digital services hosted in foreign jurisdictions.
02
Infrastructure Dependency
Critical national services increasingly depend on concentrated infrastructure — hyperscale cloud, submarine cables, and centralised data centres. A single point of failure can cascade across entire economies.
03
Digital Sovereignty
Governments are accelerating efforts to establish sovereign control over data, infrastructure, and digital services. The regulatory and operational frameworks for this transition remain undefined.

The Roundtables

Four Working Sessions

Roundtable 1
Digital Sovereignty & Data Governance
How should nations define and protect sovereign data assets in an era of distributed cloud infrastructure?
Key Discussion Areas
National data classification frameworks
Sovereign cloud requirements and certification
Cross-border data flow agreements
Digital asset protection during geopolitical disruption
Roundtable 2
Infrastructure Resilience & Continuity
What does true operational continuity look like when physical infrastructure, connectivity, and personnel face simultaneous pressure?
Key Discussion Areas
Multi-layered redundancy beyond traditional DR
Supply chain resilience for critical infrastructure
Workforce continuity under sustained disruption
Cross-sector dependency mapping
Roundtable 3
Quantum Security & Cryptographic Transition
How should governments and operators prepare cryptographic infrastructure for the post-quantum era?
Key Discussion Areas
Post-quantum cryptography migration timelines
Harvest-now-decrypt-later threat assessment
National quantum-readiness frameworks
Standards harmonisation across jurisdictions
Roundtable 4
Cloud Continuity & International Cooperation
What frameworks are needed to ensure sovereign access to cloud services during periods of geopolitical instability?
Key Discussion Areas
Bilateral and multilateral data agreements
Emergency access protocols for critical services
Hyperscaler governance and accountability
Regional infrastructure cooperation models
